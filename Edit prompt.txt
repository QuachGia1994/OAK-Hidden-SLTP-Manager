Implement a MetaTrader 5 entry guard with the following rules:

1. Determine a restricted calendar period:
   - Start date: the last Tuesday of a given month.
   - End date: the Monday immediately following the first Friday
     of the next month.
   - Both the start date and end date are inclusive.

2. Example:
   - Last Tuesday of July 2026: 2026-07-28.
   - First Friday of August 2026: 2026-08-07.
   - Monday immediately after that Friday: 2026-08-10.
   - Restricted period: 2026-07-28 through 2026-08-10 inclusive.

3. During the restricted calendar period:
   - When the MetaTrader 5 hour is H=12, status must be:
     DO NOT ENTER.
   - When the MetaTrader 5 hour is H=16, status must be:
     DO NOT ENTER.
