# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of experience, identity, background, or viewpoint. Technical disagreement is welcome; personal attacks and intimidation are not.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss code, evidence, and user impact in good faith.
- Give actionable feedback and accept correction gracefully.
- Protect private account, broker, and credential information.
- Respect maintainers' time and the safety constraints of financial software.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, spam, deliberate disclosure of secrets, and attempts to pressure others into unsafe trading actions are not permitted.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer through the contact method listed on the [maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/QuachGia1994). Reports will be reviewed confidentially. Maintainers may edit or remove content and temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

This policy applies in repository spaces and in public spaces when someone is representing the project.
